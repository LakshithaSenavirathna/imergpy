Knowledge Base

IMERG data comes in different processing speeds, affecting accuracy:

  • Early Run: (~4hr delay) Pro: Real-time. Con: Satellite only.
  • Late Run: (~14hr delay) Pro: Accurate. Con: Moderate delay.
  • Final Run: (~3.5mo delay) Pro: Gauge calibrated. Con: Historical only.

How a single Lat/Lon point is estimated from a grid of satellite data:

  • Nearest Neighbor: Pro: Raw/Exact. Con: Grid-like.
  • Linear: Pro: Smooth gradients. Con: Averaged.
  • Cubic: Pro: Visual beauty. Con: High CPU/Negatives.

You need a free NASA Earthdata account to download IMERG data.

  1. Visit the Earthdata Portal.
  2. Create your free account.
  3. IMPORTANT: After logging in, go to "Applications > Authorized Apps" and approve GES DISC to accept the data license.
